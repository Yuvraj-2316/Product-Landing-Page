GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

The useful details, without the guesswork.

Is NovaBook Pro a real product available to buy?+

No. NovaBook Pro is a fictional product concept created for a student web-design project.

Are the specifications confirmed?+

No technical specifications are being claimed on this website. The content describes the intended design direction, not verified hardware.

What happens if I sign up for product updates?+

This demo validates your email and displays a confirmation on the page. It does not send emails or save your address to a database.

Can I use the website on my phone?+

Yes. The layout and navigation are designed to adapt to smaller screens.

How can I share feedback about the concept?+

Use the product updates page for the demonstration signup flow, or contact the student project owner directly if you add your contact details.
